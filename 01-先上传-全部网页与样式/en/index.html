01 / CENTER OVERVIEW

Starting with a thorough assessment
Attending to every sleep concern

The center opened on March 30, 2025, at the Taoxi Campus of the First People's Hospital of Zunyi. It includes a sleep specialty clinic, a sleep monitoring center, and an inpatient sleep unit.

Specialists in anesthesiology, neurology, psychiatry and psychology, traditional Chinese medicine, respiratory medicine, and otolaryngology work together to assess, treat, and manage a range of sleep problems over time.

03 / CARE PATHWAY

Assessment informs care
Coordinated treatment and continuing follow-up

Assessment, monitoring, and follow-up run through our integrated care pathway. CBT-I, psychotherapy, anesthesia-related treatment, and other interventions are combined as appropriate for each person.

  1. 01

    Assess Sleep and Overall Well-Being

    Consultation, sleep diaries, questionnaires, and psychological assessment help identify sleep habits, mood, related conditions, and medication history.

  2. 02

    Monitor and Diagnose

    Polysomnography, heart rate variability analysis, and other tests are used as needed to assess sleep patterns, breathing, and autonomic nervous system function.

  3. 03

    Develop an Integrated Care Plan

    Based on assessment, a multidisciplinary team selects and combines suitable treatments, adjusting the plan with ongoing monitoring.

  4. 04

    Reassess and Follow Up

    Changes in sleep and daytime functioning, medication use, and treatment tolerance are reviewed, with home management and further assessment as needed.

I. Cognitive Behavioral, Psychological & Mind–body Treatments

01 / Cognitive & Behavioral Intervention

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I)
CBT-I

Treatment addresses sleep-related thoughts and behaviors through stimulus control, sleep restriction, cognitive strategies, and relaxation, with ongoing review of sleep diaries. Outpatient classes and inpatient learning help patients continue these practices in daily life.

Explore the Six Class Topics ↓
02 / Psychological & Mind–body Support

Psychotherapy & Mind–body Care

Care considers the links among sleep, mood, and stress. Psychological assessment informs counseling, cognitive strategies, and relaxation training, while music, reading, and group activities offer additional ways to support well-being.

Explore Care Spaces & Activities ↗
1.1 / CBT-I Classes & Practice

Bring sleep skills into everyday life

Outpatient classes cover six topics in sequence. Inpatient education, sleep diaries, relaxation exercises, and online and in-person discussion support ongoing practice.

  1. 01

    Understanding Insomnia

    Learn about factors that affect sleep and how insomnia develops and persists.

  2. 02

    Changing Sleep Behaviors

    Learn stimulus control and sleep restriction under professional guidance.

  3. 03

    Sleep Health Education

    Understand sleep hygiene and build habits that suit your daily life.

  4. 04

    Recognizing Sleep Beliefs

    Notice unrealistic expectations about sleep and related worries.

  5. 05

    Adjusting Thoughts & Emotions

    Learn cognitive restructuring and the ABC model of emotions, and practice relaxation.

  6. 06

    Coping With Recurring Insomnia

    Reinforce what you have learned and plan for changes in sleep.

Following professional assessment, treatment is delivered with sleep and vital-sign monitoring under the care of anesthesiologists and nurses, as part of integrated care for some complex sleep problems.

04 / Neuromodulation

Stellate Ganglion Block

After assessing indications, clinicians perform an ultrasound-guided stellate ganglion block, followed by observation and reassessment to guide further care.

05 / Neuromodulation

Stellate Ganglion Pulsed Radiofrequency

After assessing indications, clinicians perform stellate ganglion pulsed radiofrequency with combined DSA and ultrasound guidance, followed by observation and reassessment.

Physical Neuromodulation

Clinical assessment guides the use of repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation, transcranial electrical stimulation, and auricular vagus nerve stimulation, alongside light therapy, biofeedback, and other interventions.
