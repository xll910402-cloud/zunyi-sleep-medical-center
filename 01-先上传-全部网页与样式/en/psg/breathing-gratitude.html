A Pennant of Thanks Reflects Trust in Sleep Breathing Care

A patient who had long experienced waking up gasping at night thanked the center's care team.

It began with waking up gasping at night

A patient had been troubled for years by waking up gasping during sleep. Persistent snoring, waking up gasping, or daytime sleepiness warrants a professional consultation to determine whether further testing is needed.

The work behind a gesture of thanks

The center's team assesses sleep concerns, explains findings, and provides follow-up care. The patient's pennant was an expression of gratitude for the team's communication and care. One person's experience does not represent the treatment outcomes of every patient.

Seek a professional assessment

If you or a family member repeatedly experience abnormal breathing at night, a physician can assess whether sleep monitoring or further intervention is needed based on your clinical situation.

Further reading

Specific tests and treatments are determined by a physician based on individual circumstances.
