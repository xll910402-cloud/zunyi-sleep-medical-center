Tips for Using a Positive Airway Pressure Device

Whether the mask fits and feels comfortable are issues worth discussing throughout respiratory support.

Pay attention to mask fit

For people whose physician has recommended positive airway pressure therapy, mask fit can affect the experience of using it. Mask leaks, pressure discomfort, and dry mouth or nose can be discussed with the care team at a follow-up visit.

Adjustments during use

A professional should decide on the type of respiratory support device, pressure settings, and follow-up schedule in light of the diagnosis and experience of using the device. Do not change treatment settings on your own based solely on promotional videos.

Describe problems clearly

Recording any discomfort during use and when it occurs can help the care team determine whether the mask, humidification, or device settings need adjustment.
