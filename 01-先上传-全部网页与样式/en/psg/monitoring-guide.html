Polysomnography: From Testing to Results

Learn what polysomnography records and when it may be used.

What does the test record?

Monitoring usually covers signals related to sleep structure, breathing, heart rate, and blood oxygen. Symptoms and assessment goals vary between patients, so testing arrangements and the results of interest may also differ.

How are the results used?

Physicians analyze monitoring results alongside symptoms such as snoring, waking up gasping, and daytime sleepiness, as well as other medical history. Sleep monitoring can help assess some sleep breathing problems, but it cannot replace a full clinical consultation and judgment.

What should you do before and after testing?

Follow the hospital's actual instructions for appointments, test preparation, and collecting your report. After receiving the results, discuss them with a sleep specialist rather than focusing on a single measure.
