World Narcolepsy Day: Understanding Daytime Sleepiness

On World Narcolepsy Day, learn why persistent daytime sleepiness deserves careful assessment.

Do not assume a single cause of daytime sleepiness

Repeated, hard-to-control daytime sleepiness can have many causes. Narcolepsy is only one sleep disorder that requires professional assessment; a short video or one symptom is not enough for self-diagnosis.

Assessment requires a full picture

Physicians usually ask about sleep habits, daytime symptoms, relevant medical history, and medications, and may arrange sleep monitoring as needed. A professional will decide whether other tests are necessary.

Understanding and support

If persistent sleepiness affects learning, work, or everyday safety, seeking medical advice early is more helpful than labeling yourself.

Further reading

Specific tests and treatments are determined by a physician based on individual circumstances.
