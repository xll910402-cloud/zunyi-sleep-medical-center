Head Massage and Comprehensive Care

Difficulty falling asleep, light sleep, head tension, and daytime fatigue are common sleep concerns.

Starting with relaxation

Head massage is a nursing and relaxation service that focuses on bodily tension and winding down before sleep in a quiet setting. It is one part of the center's comprehensive services and cannot replace a professional assessment of persistent insomnia, sleep-related breathing problems, or emotional distress.

Comprehensive assessment matters

Sleep problems may be related to daily routines, stress, mood, and other physical conditions. The choice of services such as traditional Chinese medicine care or physical therapy should be discussed by professionals and the person seeking care, based on medical history, symptoms, and relevant tests.

When to visit the hospital

If sleep problems recur and affect how you feel during the day, or are accompanied by pronounced snoring, waking up gasping, or low mood, a hospital assessment is recommended. The specific plan and its effects vary from person to person.

A note about seeking care

Health education articles and patient stories provide an overview of care approaches. Specific tests and treatments should be based on a professional assessment.
