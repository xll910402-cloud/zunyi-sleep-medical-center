Snoring and Waking Up Gasping at Night

If snoring comes with waking up gasping at night, repeated awakenings, or daytime fatigue, it is worth looking more closely at nighttime breathing and sleep.

From symptoms to assessment

A person with a long history of restless sleep, snoring, and waking up gasping at night came to the center. Based on their medical history, the team arranged overnight polysomnography to examine sleep patterns, breathing events, and blood oxygen changes before assessing whether a sleep-related breathing disorder was present.

Care should be tailored to each person

Noninvasive ventilation, weight management, and behavioral guidance may be discussed as part of comprehensive care. A professional team should determine specific treatments and device settings in light of test results, physical condition, and response at follow-up. Snoring alone cannot indicate how severe a sleep-related breathing problem is.

Signs to watch for

If family members notice pauses in breathing or repeated episodes of waking up gasping at night, or if the person feels unwell in the morning or sleepy during the day, they can ask a sleep specialist whether monitoring is needed. Identifying the cause earlier can help guide discussion of suitable care.

A note about seeking care

Health education articles and patient stories provide an overview of care approaches. Specific tests and treatments should be based on a professional assessment.
