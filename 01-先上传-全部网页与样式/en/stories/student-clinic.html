About the Student Sleep Support Clinic

During exam preparation, sleep difficulties often intertwine with stress, daily routines, and emotional distress. The Student Sleep Support Clinic focuses on the sleep health of students preparing for exams.

What concerns does the clinic address?

Difficulty falling asleep, waking too early, light sleep with frequent dreams, pre-exam anxiety, and difficulty concentrating all deserve careful assessment. Sleep physicians, psychotherapists, and nurses work together to help students and parents understand possible contributing factors.

What support is available?

Services include assessment of sleep and psychological well-being, relaxation training, suggestions for self-management at home, and guidance on family communication and support. Options such as transcranial magnetic stimulation and electronic biofeedback may also be discussed after professional assessment. Whether they are suitable or needed for an individual must be determined by professionals based on the assessment findings.
