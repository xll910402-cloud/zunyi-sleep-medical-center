Ma Runxing

Associate Chief Physician

Ma Runxing is Deputy Director of the Sleep Medicine Clinical Center and an Associate Chief Physician. Ma focuses on persistent insomnia and sleep-related mental health concerns and participates in anesthesia-based sleep care and multidisciplinary interventions at the center.

PROFESSIONAL FOCUS

Areas of Practice

  • Assessment and Treatment of Persistent Insomnia
  • Ultrasound-Guided Nerve Blocks and Pulsed Radiofrequency
  • Bionic Sleep Therapy and Mental Health Services
PROFILE

Profile

Ma Runxing has undertaken advanced training at the First Affiliated Hospital of Sun Yat-sen University, Qilu Hospital of Shandong University, and the First People's Hospital of Yunnan Province. Clinical work has included ultrasound-guided nerve blocks, pulsed radiofrequency, and bionic sleep therapy. Ma Runxing also provides sleep-related psychological counseling and health education. A physician assesses which tests and treatments are appropriate for each individual.
