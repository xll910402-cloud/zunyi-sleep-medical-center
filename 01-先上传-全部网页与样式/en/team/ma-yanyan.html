Ma Yanyan

Attending Physician · Master's-level postgraduate

Ma Yanyan is an Attending Physician and master's-level postgraduate. The main areas of work are sleep-related breathing disorders and the assessment and medically guided tapering or discontinuation of sleep medications for people who have used them long term.

PROFESSIONAL FOCUS

Areas of Practice

  • Management of Sleep Medication Tapering and Discontinuation
  • Stellate Ganglion Blocks and Bionic Sleep Therapy
PROFILE

Profile

Her team profile notes advanced training in sleep medicine at the First People's Hospital of Yunnan Province, work involving stellate ganglion blocks and bionic sleep therapy, and academic exchanges in sleep-disordered breathing. For complex insomnia and medication concerns, she emphasizes individualized plans based on sleep monitoring, mind–body assessment, and follow-up.
