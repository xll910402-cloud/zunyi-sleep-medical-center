Xiong Liulin

Professor · Chief Physician

Professor Xiong Liulin is a Chief Physician and the discipline lead of the Sleep Medicine Clinical Center, leading a multidisciplinary team in sleep disorder assessment, comprehensive care, research, and training.

PROFESSIONAL FOCUS

Areas of Practice

  • Comprehensive Sleep Disorder Care
  • Research on Sleep and Co-occurring Psychiatric Conditions
  • Multidisciplinary Collaboration and Discipline Development
PROFILE

Profile

Xiong Liulin holds two doctoral degrees. Under Xiong’s leadership, the center brings together expertise in anesthesiology, neuropsychiatry, traditional Chinese medicine, and respiratory medicine. It has developed a continuum of sleep health services covering assessment, monitoring, treatment, and follow-up, while linking clinical practice, research, and teaching.
