PROFILE

Profile

Resident physician at the sleep center. Graduated from Zunyi Medical College in 2018 and has long worked in clinical care and research on sleep disorders, with a solid foundation in general medicine. Experienced in the standard assessment and treatment of insomnia disorder, obstructive sleep apnea, snoring, circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders, restless legs syndrome, narcolepsy, and REM sleep behavior disorder. Proficient in interpreting polysomnography (PSG), home sleep apnea testing, noninvasive positive airway pressure titration, and cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I).
